# The commit rule at 100% in use1 (2026-11-19)

Not a one-way deploy, and no ordering constraint.

- **What changes:** `memory_commit_pct` in `use1` goes from 150 to 100: a host's sandboxes are declared no more than its memory less its reserve. An `r8id.large` holds three default sandboxes (was five), an `m8id.large` or `m8i.large` one (was two).
- **Why:** between 2026-11-18 22:45 and 2026-11-19 05:48 UTC the kernel killed a sandbox's VMM 13 times on 8 GiB hosts that held two 4 GiB sandboxes, each time with both at 3.4 to 4.0 GiB (host journals under `logs/`). Each death was detected and the sandbox placed again within seconds, but it lost what it wrote since its last commit.
- **Capacity and cost:** with `maxHosts` 12 the cell runs 12 default sandboxes at once on 8 GiB hosts (8 on the on-demand fallback, which the account's 16 on-demand vCPUs stop at 8 hosts), 36 on `r8id.large`, which had no spot room on 2026-11-18: so expect 12, down from 24, at twice the host cost per running sandbox. `maxHosts` 12 was sized at 150%; more needs a quota increase. A placement with no room waits and is retried, as at capacity.
- **Suspended sandboxes move more:** a placement that fits nowhere moves a suspended sandbox off before a host is launched, and at one sandbox per 8 GiB host that is most placements. Such a sandbox wakes from its head on whatever host has room, not in place.
- **Hosts that already hold two** keep both: nothing evicts or drains on a settings change, so such a host can still lose a VM until one of its sandboxes ends or is suspended and moved. It takes nothing new meanwhile. An operator's drain (`POST /v1/admin/hosts/<id>/drain`) moves them at once.
- **What a rollback does:** 150% again.

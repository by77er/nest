# The commit rule at 100% in use1 (2026-11-19)

Not a one-way deploy, and no ordering constraint.

- **What changes:** `memory_commit_pct` in `use1` goes from 150 to 100: a host's sandboxes are declared no more than its memory less its reserve. An `r8id.large` holds three default sandboxes (was five), an `m8id.large` or `m8i.large` one (was two).
- **Why:** between 2026-11-18 13:58 and 2026-11-19 05:48 UTC the kernel killed a sandbox's VMM 13 times on 8 GiB hosts that held two 4 GiB sandboxes, each time with both at 3.4 to 4.0 GiB (host journals under `logs/`). Each death was detected and the sandbox placed again, but it lost what it wrote since its last commit.
- **Capacity and cost:** with `maxHosts` 12 the cell runs 12 default sandboxes at once on 8 GiB hosts, 36 on `r8id.large`; suspended ones move off when room is needed. A placement with no room waits and is retried, as at capacity.
- **Sandboxes already placed** stay where they are: a host holding two keeps both until one ends or is suspended and moved; it takes no third.
- **What a rollback does:** 150% again.

# ChannelDO

The authority for one [channel](../../../channels/): its log, keyed view, grant list, subscriptions and their cursors.

- **Storage:** message index, keyed view and recent bodies in SQLite. Bodies over 32 KiB, and every body older than 7 days, go to R2 (`channels/<channel>/b/<id>`, a random id: the object is written before the post's `seq` is assigned); reads fetch them transparently. Retention and tiering run from the alarm (daily `maintenance`). Expiry works in pages of 1000 rows (at most 10 a run, the rest a minute later): a page's bodies are deleted from R2 first (at most 1000 keys a call, R2's limit), then its rows, so a row never loses its reference while its body exists.
- **Post:**
  1. Check the poster's permission (the caller's identity, the channel's own grant list, the caller's capability grants: one pure decision, `permits` in `control-plane/src/lib/channels.ts`). The decision is made on the channel's metadata without writing anything, so **a refused call makes no channel, directory entry or alarm**; a built-in channel's object is made on its first permitted post or subscription (a read of one never posted to answers empty and makes nothing).
  2. A body over 32 KiB is put in R2 first; then, in one synchronous step, the permission is checked again (the grant list may have changed during the put), the guards run, `seq` is assigned, the message and keyed view are written, matching `turn` subscriptions open their batch window, and the alarm is armed.
  3. Return. **Fan-out never runs in the post path,** so one slow subscriber can't stall a post or keep the channel billed.
- **`from` and `taint` come from the caller,** never the body: the body schema is strict, so a post naming either is refused. AgentDO passes the agent's identity and its session's taint, the operator API passes `user`, and AgentDO and SandboxDO post their events as `platform`.
- **Guards** (`decidePost`), in order, before `seq` is assigned:
  - idempotency keys, per poster (a poster's repeated key returns its `seq`; kept 7 days);
  - `reply_to` must name an earlier message;
  - compare-and-set on the key (`if_seq`; `0` means absent), refused with `conflict` (409) and the key's current `seq`;
  - a sender's identical repeat within a minute (not stored; its earlier `seq` is returned). It never swallows a state change: a post with `if_seq` is never a repeat, and a keyed post is one only while the key still points at the sender's previous message (claim, release, re-claim lands again);
  - the per-poster rate limit (120 posts a minute per channel; not for `user` or `platform`).

  See [limits](../../../channels/#limits-and-loop-guards).
- **Permissions:** the built-in rules, a free channel's owner, the grant list, or a capability grant. Grant patterns' wildcards (`*`, `**`) match free channels only: `user` and the built-in channels are reached by the built-in rules, `{self}`/`{owner}`/`{child}`, or a grant naming them exactly.
- **Fan-out runs from the alarm** (`fanout`): `AgentDO.deliverChannel` per due subscription, 8 at a time, with a 30 s call timeout. At least once and in order per subscription: the batch is **pinned** (stored with a 35 s backstop retry) before the call, every retry carries the same range, the cursor moves only after AgentDO acknowledged it (to the batch's end, or to `up_to` when it took only part: the rest is planned again at once), and an answer counts only against the batch still pinned. A failed call backs off (1 s doubling to 5 min); AgentDO's loop guards answer `defer`, which unpins without moving the cursor, so later matches coalesce into the next batch. A subscriber that isn't an agent is dropped. Past about 100 subscribers a channel would shard its fan-out (or use Queues).
- **Subscriptions live here** (filter, delivery, batch window, `once`, cursor, the pinned batch). AgentDO keeps only its own list with each one's delivered high-water `seq`, for display and deduplication. A subscriber needs `read`; an agent subscribes only itself (the operator may subscribe any agent). The cursor starts at the head: only later messages are delivered.
  - `turn`: matches open a window of `batch_ms` (default 2 s, at most 10 min); everything matching in the window is one batch.
  - `none`: no delivery; the subscriber's `read` without `since` reads from the cursor and moves it (a re-run of the same tool call reads the same page again).
  - `once`: the first match is the batch, and the subscription ends when AgentDO takes it.
- **Built-in channels** (`<agt>/inbox`, `<agt>/events`, `<sbx>/events`, `user`) exist implicitly. An agent's inbox has its agent's subscription (`sub_inbox`, `turn`, from the start of the log), which nobody can remove (it is made once, with the channel). An agent's events gets its spawner's subscription (`sub_spawner`, kinds `turn_completed` and `exited`) on an event AgentDO posts, **only if the spawner's spec has the `read` tool**: a notice tells the model to read, so a spawner that can't (the seed templates) gets no subscription, and deploying channels changes nothing for it. It is marked made only when made, and the child re-checks an owner's `read` every 10 minutes, so an owner that gains it is subscribed at the next event. Free channels are made by `channel_create` (the creator owns them; idempotent for the owner, refused for anyone else) or the operator; names starting with `agt_`, `sbx_`, `hk_`, `ws_`, `user/` or `platform`, and `ledger` and `ledger/…` (a [task ledger](../tasks/)'s channel), are reserved.
- **Directory:** each channel registers itself (from its alarm, retried) with the `~directory` instance, which the operator API lists.
- **Cost:** idle channels hold no WebSocket and no alarm beyond the daily maintenance, so they cost only storage.

**Built** (control-plane `src/do/channel.ts`, every decision in `src/lib/channels.ts`, unit- and property-tested): all of the above.

**Not built:**
- `await_reply_s` and `read` with a wait (the `once` subscription they rely on is built);
- hooks posting (HookDO), the guest `nest post`, the `interrupt` flag, the wake envelope's budget line;
- background task exits on `<agt>/inbox` (they still reach a working brain's inbox directly);
- a route to change a channel's retention (set at creation, `retention_days`, default 30; built-in channels use the default).

**Stage 2 (planned, not built):** every agent gets `read` on its own built-in channels (its inbox, its children's events) whatever its template, so the `read` gate above stops mattering, and each agent is told about the notices it was not shown (AgentDO's `channel_subs.skipped` and `skipped_to` per subscription: the range is still in the channel, within its retention); existing children are subscribed for their spawners at their next event; `notify`, `ask`, `emit`, the ledger's notices and `POST /v1/agents/<id>/inbox` move from AgentDO's inbox and the interim [workstream channel](../../../orchestration/workstreams/#the-interim-workstream-channel) onto channels. Until then the operator reaches any agent with `POST /v1/agents/<id>/inbox`, or with a post to `<agt>/inbox`: an agent without `read` gets the operator's own text inline, every message whole (across turns when they don't fit one notice), and one too big to keep inline (over 32 KiB, its body in R2) as a note that it needs `read`.

**Garbage collection** (built 2026-11-02): a channel collects itself, so many short-lived agents leave nothing behind.
- **When:** at a `maintenance` run that leaves its log empty (retention took every message; a keyed message is never taken, so a keyed channel stays), if what it belongs to is gone. That is an agent's records pruned (`unknown_agent`, 30 days after its end) for `<agt>/inbox` and `<agt>/events`; the sandbox destroyed (or unknown) for `<sbx>/events`; and, for a free channel an agent owns, that agent and every subscriber gone. `user` and the operator's free channels never go. The checks use only RPC methods every release has (`info`), so the [post-deploy window](../../#upgrades) can't misread a live subject as gone.
- **How:** its R2 bodies (`channels/<name>/`), then its directory row, then its storage and alarm. A post that lands during those awaits keeps it (registered again). A later permitted use makes it afresh.
- **An empty channel whose subject lives** is checked again weekly instead of daily; a post re-arms the daily run.
- **Quota:** an agent owns at most 100 free channels (`MAX_OWNED_CHANNELS`), counted from the directory, so `channel_create` past it answers `capacity_exhausted`; its own re-create of one it owns still answers. The count lags a new channel's registration, so a burst can pass it by a few. The operator is uncapped.
